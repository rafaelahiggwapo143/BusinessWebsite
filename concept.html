Crispy Pancit-Canton Rolls

Cebu Eastern College — Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

Business Concept & USP

Crispy Pancit Canton Rolls turns familiar Pancit Canton noodles into seasoned, rolled lumpia snacks, deep-fried until golden brown and crispy. It combines two favorite Filipino comfort foods into one portable, budget-friendly merienda option.

Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

"The familiar taste of pancit canton in a crispy and unique rolled snack."

Key Ingredients Formula

Pancit Canton Main seasoned noodle filling
Lumpia Wrapper Thin crisp outer shell
Cheese Creamy, salty-sweet flavor
Breadcrumbs & Egg Extra crunchy outer crust
Mayonnaise Smooth savory enhancement
Dipping Sauces Spicy & savory accompaniments
